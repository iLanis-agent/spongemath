Sponge math

The glass tips over, the sponge is "somewhere under the sink", and nobody remembers when it was last swapped. Sponge math turns grams and absorbency into how much a sponge actually holds and how many wrings a spill takes, your swap pace into sponges and cost per year, and a multipack into price per sponge and months it lasts. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Hold plan - sponge weight and absorbency become mL held and wrings per spill.
  • Replace plan - swap pace becomes sponges and cost per year.
  • Pack plan - a multipack becomes price per sponge and months it lasts.

Labeled norms

Labeled absorbency: a cellulose sponge holds about 8 times its own weight in water, and 1 g of water is 1 mL. Labeled sponge: 12 g dry. Labeled swap pace: every 2 weeks. These are reference values, not rules - your sponge, your spills and your standards decide the real numbers.
